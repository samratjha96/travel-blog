Some links on Techbro Travels are **affiliate links**. If you click one and make a booking or purchase, I earn a small commission — at **no extra cost to you**. The price you pay is exactly the same as if you had gone to the site directly.

#### The one rule that matters

**I only link to things I have personally done, stayed in, or used.**

If a post recommends a hot air balloon operator, it's because I flew with them. If it links to a hotel, it's because I slept there. I don't link to tours I haven't taken, hotels I haven't stayed in, or services I haven't used — no exceptions. If I can't vouch for it from personal experience, it doesn't get a link.

#### How affiliate links work here

- Affiliate links look like any other link. Some are marked with a note like "(affiliate link)" when it helps clarity.
- Commissions typically range from a few percent of the booking value up to 20–40% depending on the partner (hotels, tours, insurance).
- I earn from bookings made through these links whether you use them the same day or weeks later (most partner programs use a tracking cookie of 14–30 days).
- Cost data in my posts is never adjusted to favor an affiliate. If a place is expensive, I say so — that's the whole point of this blog.

#### Programs I participate in

As links are added, this section lists the affiliate programs currently in use (for example: booking platforms, tour marketplaces, and travel insurance providers). Each program pays a commission on completed bookings made through links on this site.

#### What this doesn't change

- Rankings, price breakdowns, and verdicts are written **before** any monetization is considered.
- Nothing on this site is sponsored or paid placement. If that ever changes, it will be disclosed inline, in the same clear way.
- No post is written because a company asked for it. Brands don't get input, previews, or approval over my content.

#### FTC compliance

This disclosure is provided in line with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's 16 CFR Part 255 (Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising): affiliate relationships are disclosed clearly and conspicuously on every page where they appear, and every endorsement reflects my genuine, first-hand experience.
